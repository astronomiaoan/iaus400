📅 June 8. Posters must be printed in A1 size (841 mm × 594 mm; 33.1″ × 23.4″) and preferably landscape orientation. Due to the high number of poster, this format allows for two posters to be displayed on each side of the poster boards and ensures that all contributions can be accommodated within the main hall of the venue. Please remember to bring your printed poster to the conference. If needed, local printing services are available near the venue in El Poblado, Medellín, with prices around 10 USD. Option here
