Registration

Registration for the symposium will open on February 20, 2025. Early registration is highly encouraged to secure your spot at this event, which promises to be an unparalleled gathering of leading researchers and professionals in solar and stellar physics.
You will have the option to register at a reduced rate if you complete your registration before the Early Registration Deadline on April 30, 2025. After this date, regular registration fees will apply. Please note that registration will close on July 1, 2025, so we recommend registering early to take full advantage of all the symposium benefits, including access to all sessions.

The registration fee includes:
✔️ Welcome reception
✔️ Access to all conference sessions
✔️ Coffee breaks
✔️ Conference materials
✔️ Electronic version of the proceedings
✔️ City tour
✔️ Night bus tour
✔️ It does not include the conference dinner (Thursday) or the full-day coffee experience tour (Saturday).

👉 Regular Registration (until July 1): 1600000 COP (1400000 COP for students)

📌 Please note: 1 USD is approximately 4129 Colombian Pesos (COP).
When proceeding to the payment platform, please ensure that the amount to be paid is entered in COP (Colombian Pesos).


⚠️ We are pleased to offer special registration rates for certain participants, made possible through internal and external funding sources.
- Students and researchers residing in Colombia: Enter the code (identificador): COL-IAUS400
- Members of the international collaboration DynaSun : To obtain the promotional code please contact the collaboration coordinator at the node to which they belong.

These special codes allow us to provide financial support to these groups through dedicated funding channels





For any questions regarding registration, abstract submissions, or student assistance, please feel free to contact our team through the Contact page. We are here to help ensure your participation in this exciting event is as smooth as possible.
